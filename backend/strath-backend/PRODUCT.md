# StrathSpace Admin Console

## Product Register

### Register: product

- **Users:** Trusted StrathSpace operators managing a student matchmaking community. They need to review users, matches, payments, feedback, and rollout health quickly and accurately.
- **Purpose:** Give administrators a dependable operational view of StrathSpace, with the context and controls needed to support users and improve matchmaking quality.
- **Personality:** Calm, precise, discreet, and trustworthy.
- **Anti-references:** Consumer dating-app theatrics, decorative glass or gradients that reduce readability, oversized marketing layouts, and dense enterprise tables without hierarchy.
- **Core principles:**
  1. Put the operator's next decision and supporting evidence together.
  2. Favor scannable, readable data over decoration.
  3. Use consistent navigation, filters, states, and terminology across admin tools.
  4. Make sensitive user information clear but visually restrained.
  5. Design every state: loading, empty, error, filtered, and populated.
- **Accessibility:** Semantic headings and tables, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, descriptive labels, and responsive layouts that remain usable on narrow screens.
